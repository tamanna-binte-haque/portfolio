Some Highlighted Projects

Strategic Information Systems Planning

Developed a Strategic Information Systems Plan for Yoobee Colleges NZ, evaluating fragmented legacy systems (SMS, Blackboard LMS) and proposing a TOGAF-aligned, cloud-based integrated architecture. Also conducted a cultural and ethical analysis of an IS rollout, applying Te Tiriti o Waitangi principles and Privacy Act 2020 compliance to recommend a culturally responsive co-design framework.
Information Systems Analysis & Design

This project involved analyzing an existing system's requirements to identify gaps and inefficiencies, followed by designing a proposed solution using UML and ER diagrams to model system structure and data flow. It strengthened my ability to move from requirements analysis to structured technical design across the systems development lifecycle.
Database Management Systems

Designed EER/ER diagrams and normalized a real estate database schema from unnormalised form through 3NF, then critically reviewed and redesigned a flawed college database schema — adding referential integrity, composite keys, and indexing strategies. Applied Privacy Act 2020 considerations to database design, including data isolation and role-based access control for sensitive customer information.
